🧾工艺与历史 / Technique and History

中国雕版印刷已有一千多年的历史。传统工艺将文字与图案反向刻在木板上，保留凸起的印刷部分，再上墨、覆纸、施压，将图像转印到纸张。同一块雕版可以反复印制，让文字、图像与知识更广泛地传播。

唐代868年的《金刚经》，是现存最早有明确纪年的完整印刷书。北宋毕昇发展了可拆分重排的泥活字，而雕版印刷仍长期延续。2009年，中国雕版印刷技艺列入联合国教科文组织人类非物质文化遗产代表作名录。

Chinese woodblock printing has a history of more than a thousand years. Artisans carve a design in reverse on a wooden block, leaving the printing surface raised. Ink is applied, paper is laid over the block, and pressure transfers the image. A single block can produce many impressions, helping texts, images and knowledge reach more people.

The Diamond Sutra, dated 868 CE, is the earliest surviving complete printed book with an explicit date. In the Northern Song dynasty, Bi Sheng developed reusable clay movable type, while woodblock printing continued for centuries. In 2009, China’s engraved block printing technique was inscribed on UNESCO’s Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

🧧图案中的故事与祝愿 / Stories and Good Wishes

这次作品将知识传承与文化祝愿放在一起：孔子的形象表达对教育与先贤的敬意；青花瓷纹样中的双鱼环绕福字，寓意年年有余、福气相伴；龙纹盖罐以盘曲的中国龙，传达祥瑞与护佑的文化意象。

一对青龙与白虎石刻图像呈现传统守护意象：青龙对应东方，白虎对应西方。它们在本次展示中寄托平安与成功的祝愿，供来宾观赏。

四款滚轴作品加入当代趣味。“马上幸福”与“马上暴富”利用马上的双重含义——立即与在马背上——把祝福变成图文游戏；“马到成功”祝愿迅速成功、事业顺遂；“保护知识产权就是保护创新”则以盾牌、灯泡、书本和机器人，将传统印刷与今天的创意、知识和技术相联系。

The designs bring learning and good wishes together. Confucius honours education and the passing on of knowledge. Two fish surrounding the character fú (福) express wishes for abundance year after year and good fortune: the Chinese words for fish and surplus share the same pronunciation, yú. A dragon-decorated covered jar carries associations of auspiciousness and protection.

The Azure Dragon and White Tiger stone-carved images represent traditional guardians of the east and west. Displayed as a pair, they carry our wishes for protection, peace and success. These two works are for viewing.

Four roller-operated designs add a contemporary touch. Happiness, Right Away! and Strike It Rich! play on mǎshàng, which means both right away and on horseback. Swift Success translates the wish expressed by mǎ dào chéng gōng. Protect IP, Protect Innovation uses a shield, light bulb, books and robot to connect printing with creativity, knowledge and technology today.

拓印四步 / Rubbing Print in Four Steps

适用于孔子、青花瓷和龙纹盖罐三款。四款滚轴作品使用滚轴操作印制。
For the Confucius, Porcelain and Dragon Jar designs. The four roller designs are printed with a roller.

  1. 在雕版上刷颜料Brush colour onto the carved block
  2. 覆上一张宣纸Lay a sheet of xuan (rice) paper over it
  3. 用拓包轻压，让图案转到纸上Press with a cloth rubbing pad (tuobao) to pick up the image
  4. 从雕版上揭下宣纸，作品完成Peel the paper off the block to reveal your print

先师孔子行教像Portrait of Confucius

可自行印制 · Make Your Own Print

这件作品以端庄的孔子形象，表达对先贤与教育的敬意。印样题有“先师孔子行教像”，并以“德侔天地，道冠古今；删述六经，垂宪万世”的文字颂扬其学问与德行。通过雕版印刷，人物形象与文字一同留在纸上，让知识传承成为可见、可分享的故事。

This dignified portrait of Confucius honours learning and the passing on of knowledge. Its inscriptions praise his wisdom, virtue and lasting influence as a teacher. Printing brings the portrait and its words together on paper, giving this tribute a form that visitors can share.

青花瓷·双鱼纳福Porcelain Motif: Fish and Good Fortune

可自行印制 · Make Your Own Print

这件作品以瓷瓶为轮廓，双鱼环绕中央的“福”字，周围饰以花卉纹样。“鱼”与“余”谐音，寄托“年年有余”的祝愿；“福”字则代表福气与好运。两种吉祥意象相伴，表达生活丰足、福气常在的美好心愿。

Within the outline of a porcelain vase, two fish surround the character fú (福), meaning good fortune or blessings. The Chinese words for fish and surplus share the pronunciation yú, so fish express a wish for abundance year after year. Together, the fish and fú offer wishes for a plentiful life and lasting good fortune.

青龙与白虎Azure Dragon and White Tiger

仅供观赏 · Viewing Only

“左青龙，右白虎”呈现中国传统的守护意象。这一对作品以龙的盘曲姿态和虎的昂扬身形，寄托护佑、平安与成功的祝愿。青龙与白虎石刻图像在本次展览中成对展示，供来宾观赏。

The Azure Dragon and White Tiger are traditional Chinese guardian symbols, often paired as the dragon on the left and the tiger on the right. Their flowing, energetic forms carry wishes for protection, peace and success. This pair of stone-carving images is displayed for viewing.

中国龙·龙纹盖罐Chinese Dragon on a Covered Jar

可自行印制 · Make Your Own Print

这件作品以有盖罐为外形，将一条盘曲的中国龙融入器身装饰。龙在中国传统文化中是祥瑞的象征，常与护佑、幸福以及江河湖泊等水域相联系。龙纹也是中国陶瓷的传统装饰题材。盘曲的龙身与繁密的纹饰相结合，呈现出鲜明的动势。

This woodblock print depicts a coiling Chinese dragon within the outline of a covered jar. In Chinese tradition, dragons are auspicious creatures associated with protection, happiness and water. Dragon motifs are also a familiar feature of Chinese ceramics. The winding body and richly patterned decoration give the image a lively sense of movement.

马上幸福Happiness, Right Away!

可自行印制 · Make Your Own Print滚轴操作 · Roller Printing

马与福字共同表达幸福和好运的祝愿。马上既指立即，也可按字面理解为在马背上，形成图文双关，寓意幸福即刻到来。

The horse and the Chinese character fú (福), meaning good fortune or blessings, offer a wish for happiness and good luck. Mǎshàng means both “right away” and “on horseback,” creating a playful wish for happiness to arrive without delay.

马到成功Swift Success

可自行印制 · Make Your Own Print滚轴操作 · Roller Printing

马到成功是祝愿迅速取得成功的成语，字面意为马一到便成功。画中的马寄托对新事业、新目标顺利成功的祝福。

Mǎ dào chéng gōng is a Chinese idiom meaning “swift success,” literally “success as soon as the horse arrives.” This spirited horse carries a wish for success in your next venture.

马上暴富Strike It Rich!

可自行印制 · Make Your Own Print滚轴操作 · Roller Printing

马与钱币把马上的双重含义结合起来，幽默地表达迅速发财的祝愿。马上暴富是夸张而轻松的吉祥祝福。

The horse and coins turn the double meaning of mǎshàng—“right away” and “on horseback”—into a cheerful wish to strike it rich. This is a light-hearted blessing for wealth and prosperity!

保护知识产权就是保护创新Protect IP, Protect Innovation

可自行印制 · Make Your Own Print滚轴操作 · Roller Printing

盾牌与锁象征保护，灯泡、齿轮、书本和机器人表现创意、知识和技术。作品以保护知识产权就是保护创新为主题，致意让新想法成为现实的创造者。

Protecting intellectual property means protecting innovation. The shield and lock represent protection, while the light bulb, gears, books and robot evoke creativity, knowledge and technology. The design celebrates the people whose imagination and work bring new ideas to life.
